FAQ

Questions, answered

How many revisions do I get?

2 rounds are included in every package. Extra rounds can be added for a small fee.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, bank transfer and PayPal. 50% advance, 50% on delivery.

How long does a project take?

Most logos take 3 to 5 days and most animations 2 to 7 days. The exact time is in each service.

What files will I receive?

PNG, SVG and PDF for design, MP4 or GIF for animation. Source files can be shared on request.

Can I get a refund?

If work has not started, the advance is refundable. Once drafts are shared, the advance covers the time spent.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I can invoice in other currencies and we can chat on WhatsApp or email.
